## What happened

Engaged counter, kept. Conflicting schemas reduce recall. Forcing two incompatible groupings into one schema page makes the hot cue and the gist point at a claim the memories no longer share. Source: https://exa.ai/library/publication/1fyvnft1wn1

The cascade rule answers this without deleting the counter. When memories contradict, gist and schema must change. A second schema is allowed when the subject has actually changed, not as a way to hide a contradiction inside one folder.
