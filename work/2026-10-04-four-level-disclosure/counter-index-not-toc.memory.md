## What happened

Engaged counter, kept. An index note should not list every note on a topic. It is an entry point. About 25 links is a practical ceiling; past that, split the topic. Source: https://notes.sebastianauner.com/docs/03-resources/01-zettelkasten/public/index-notes-are-not-a-table-of-contents/

Applied here: index.md lists schemas that are easy to find. Memories and gists are reached by walking, not by dumping them into the index. What falls off the index is long-term, not deleted.
