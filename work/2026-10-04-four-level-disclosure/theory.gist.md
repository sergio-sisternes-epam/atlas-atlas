## Content

Index lists schemas that are still easy to find. It is not laboratory short-term memory and not a table of contents. Schema groups related gists. A folder may contain more than one schema when the subject changes. Type suffixes are the search handles: schema pages use .schema.md, gist pages use .gist.md, and memory pages use .memory.md. The reserved index.md and hub.md stay unsuffixed. These suffixes are required even when names would not collide. Gist summarises a group of memories. Memory is the detailed sequence and feeds the levels above it. A contradiction cascades upward. Same-level links stay at one level and may cross clusters.
