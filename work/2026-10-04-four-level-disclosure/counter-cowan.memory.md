## What happened

Engaged counter, kept. Cowan (2001) argues that the pure short-term capacity, when chunking and long-term recoding are blocked, averages about four chunks in the focus of attention. That is a laboratory limit, not a name for a directory listing. Source: https://memory.psych.missouri.edu/assets/doc/articles/2001/cowan-bbs-2001.pdf

Sergio corrected the earlier label. The index is the hot cue list of schemas that are easy to find. It is not Cowan's short-term memory. Long-term memory is whatever has fallen off that list. The ~25 entry-point budget and Cowan's four chunks are different claims and must not be fused.
