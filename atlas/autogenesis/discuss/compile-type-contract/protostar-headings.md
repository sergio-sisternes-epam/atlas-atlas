## Content

Old K / L / M asked which *words* to put after `##`. That is another stamp. Challenge + your last pin: stamps rot; KVA and links direct traffic; keep signals small.

Reworked choices:

**N — compile checks links, not heading names.**  
A protostar must point at where it came from and, if it has a work id, at the work page. Headings are for humans. Pending, Growth path, Open question — all fine. Template can still *suggest* Pending and Origin.

**O — one official pair of headings (old K).**  
Must say `## Pending` and `## Origin`. Consistent checklist. Old pages warn until renamed.

**P — allow either heading pair (old M).**  
Pending/Origin or Growth path/Open question. Two dialects in the graph.

**Pin (operator 2026-08-27): N.**  
Compile checks origin and work links. Heading names are optional. Template may still suggest Pending and Origin.
