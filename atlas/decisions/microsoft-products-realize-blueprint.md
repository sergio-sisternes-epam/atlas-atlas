## Decision

Microsoft knowledge products are the **enterprise-grade realization** of the Atlas blueprint. They are not the competitive set.

## Rationale

Organisations that already pay for Azure need permissioned search, graph maps, and agentic retrieval over estate sources. Those jobs should consume compile-green Atlas pages. Making them the birthplace of claims would recreate the ring-fenced pool Atlas exists to avoid.

## Alternatives considered

- Compete with Foundry IQ on recall benchmarks — rejected.
- Require Azure for any Atlas — rejected; local grep path stays default.

## Consequences

Azure work is adapter work with a content hash back to git. Do not mix it into the git-mesh storage discussion (query remains a separate conversation).
