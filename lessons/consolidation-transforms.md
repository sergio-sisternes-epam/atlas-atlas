## Content

Systems consolidation is not a file copy from hippocampus to cortex. Detail-rich episodes become coarser gist. Medial prefrontal cortex holds schematic structure. Posterior hippocampus keeps more local detail; anterior hippocampus more global context. Those representations can coexist and stay in flux.

Schemas speed integration of congruent new items into neocortex, usually at the cost of specificity. Sleep physiology adds a third player (entorhinal cortex) and at least one account treats offline activity as uncluttering irrelevant residue so salient traces stay easy to hit.

## Implication for Atlas

A compile or dream pass may coarsen, merge, or demote pages. That is transformation, not tampering, if the episodic originals remain addressable and the current-theory slot records that it superseded them.
