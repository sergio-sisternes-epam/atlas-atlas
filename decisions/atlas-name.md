## Decision

The rebooted operational knowledge skill is named **Atlas**.

## Rationale

Atlas best signals compiled, modular, navigable knowledge for skills and projects without process theatre.

## Alternatives considered

Codex, library, archive, memory, and wiki-derived names were rejected for confusion or scope mismatch.

## Consequences

Skill package, CLI (`atlas`), and stores use this name; okf remains pure format authority.
