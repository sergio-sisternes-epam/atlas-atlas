## Content

**Mission.** Atlas puts order on chaos: how we expand, navigate, and control the graph. It does not police how a page is written.

**In scope for schema and compile:** frontmatter, navigation rules (`relates_to`, KVA, work hubs), indexing, composition (how pages join).

**Out of scope:** required markdown headings and body outline. Templates may suggest headings. Compile must not fail or warn on heading names. Pin N is the protostar case of this rule.

**Implication for Stage 1.** `templates.by_type` may keep *recommended* sections for authors. Required sections are not a compile rule. Required *frontmatter* and required *edges* stay in.
